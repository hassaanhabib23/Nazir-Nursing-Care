Questions families ask us

Can't find your answer? Contact us and we will help.

How quickly can you send a nurse?

Contact us with the patient's details and your area. We will tell you how soon a nurse can reach you. For urgent needs we do our best to arrange care the same day.

Are your nurses qualified?

Yes. We check qualifications, CNIC and references before a nurse or attendant joins our team. Tell us the kind of care you need and we will match the right skills.

Can I ask for a female nurse?

Yes. Ask when you contact us and we will arrange a female nurse or attendant wherever possible.

What is the difference between a nurse and an attendant?

A registered nurse gives medical care such as injections, drips and wound care. An attendant helps with daily needs such as bathing, meals and moving around. Many families use both.

Can I book for only a few days?

Yes. You can book for a single visit, a few days or a long-term arrangement. Monthly bookings have a lower daily rate.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Islamabad and Rawalpindi, including the main Islamabad sectors, Bahria Town, DHA, Satellite Town and Saddar. If your area is not listed, contact us and ask.

How do I pay?

We agree the rate before care starts. Payment can be made in cash or by bank transfer. Ask us for details when you book.

What if the nurse is not a good fit?

Tell us and we will arrange a replacement as quickly as we can. Your patient's comfort comes first.
