Care we provide

One nurse for a few hours, or a full care team around the clock.

  • Home nursing

    Day, night or 24-hour nurses for patients who need regular medical attention.

  • Elderly care

    Patient attendants who help with daily routine, meals, hygiene and mobility.

  • After surgery

    Dressing changes, pain management and safe recovery after you leave the hospital.

  • Injections and drips

    IV drips, injections, catheter and feeding tube care at home.

  • Mother and newborn care

    Support for new mothers and care for babies in the first weeks.

  • Home ICU setup

    Oxygen, monitoring and trained staff for patients who need intensive care.

How to get a nurse

  1. Contact us

    Tell us who needs care, what the doctor has advised and where you are.

  2. We match a nurse

    We confirm the right skills, shift and rate with you before anyone is sent.

  3. Care begins at home

    Your nurse arrives, takes over from your family and keeps you updated.

What you can expect from us

  • Checked credentialsWe verify qualifications, CNIC and references before a nurse joins our team.
  • Female nurses availableAsk for a female nurse or attendant. We will arrange it wherever possible.
  • Clear rates up frontYou agree the shift and the price before care starts. No surprises later.
  • You stay informedYour family gets regular updates on how the patient is doing.
