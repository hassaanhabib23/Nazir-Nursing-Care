Our services

Care that fits the patient's needs, whether that is a few hours a day or round the clock.

Home nursing

Registered nurses who provide medical care at your home on a day, night or 24-hour shift.

  • Vital signs, medication and treatment as the doctor prescribes
  • Monitoring for changes in the patient's condition
  • Shifts of 8 hours, 12 hours or 24 hours

Elderly care

Trained attendants who look after older family members with patience and respect.

  • Help with bathing, dressing, toileting and meals
  • Mobility support and fall prevention
  • Company and a regular daily routine

Care after surgery

Safe recovery at home once the hospital discharges your patient.

  • Dressing changes and wound care
  • Pain and medication management
  • Help with walking, positioning and gentle exercise

Injections, drips and procedures

Medical procedures done at home by qualified nurses, so the patient does not need to travel.

  • Injections and IV drips
  • Catheter and feeding tube care
  • Blood sugar and blood pressure checks

Mother and newborn care

Support for mothers after delivery and care for babies in the first weeks.

  • Newborn bathing, feeding and cord care
  • Rest and recovery support for the mother
  • Guidance for first-time parents

Home ICU setup

For patients who need intensive care but are better off at home.

  • Oxygen, suction and monitor setup
  • Trained staff for ventilator and critical care
  • Coordination with your treating doctor
Not sure what your family member needs? Contact us and describe the situation. We will tell you honestly what level of care makes sense.
