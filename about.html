About Nazir Nursing Care

We help families look after the people they love, at home, with trained professionals they can trust.

Why we started

Looking after a sick or elderly family member is hard, and finding a reliable nurse should not add to the stress. Nazir Nursing Care was set up to make that simple.

We place qualified nurses and trained attendants with families across Islamabad and Rawalpindi, and we stay in touch to make sure the care is right.
